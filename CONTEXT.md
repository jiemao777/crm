# Foreign-trade CRM

This context tracks buyer enquiries from first contact through quotation, sampling and order completion.

## Language

**Inquiry**:
A buyer's request for products, pricing or custom work, tracked from receipt to a won, lost or unqualified outcome.
_Avoid_: Deal, opportunity

**Quotation**:
A versioned commercial offer for an Inquiry, including line items, trade terms, validity and status.
_Avoid_: Quote record

**Sample**:
A physical product sample prepared and shipped while qualifying an Inquiry.
_Avoid_: Trial

**Sales Order**:
The confirmed commercial order that follows a successful Inquiry and tracks production, shipment and delivery.
_Avoid_: Deal
